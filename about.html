Since 2024

From passion project to creative haven

Since 2024, we have been crafting vibrant, tactile experiences in the heart of Norfolk. Our studio began as a simple passion project, but it quickly grew into a beloved creative haven for children and families alike.

We believe that play is the best teacher, and our mission is to provide a safe, fun, and high-quality environment where every child can explore their imagination through the magic of slime.
